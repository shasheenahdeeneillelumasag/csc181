## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
I am designing for visitors attending the festival, specifically in Mugna. These visitors may need help finding vendor stalls, checking the products they sell, and viewing their opening hours and current availability.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
Visitors may need support because they may have difficulty finding stalls and knowing which vendors are open. They may also want to learn what products each vendor sells before visiting. 

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
Visitors are trying to explore the festival, find vendor stalls, and discover the products being sold. They want to locate specific stalls easily, check which vendors are open, and decide which stalls they would like to visit.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
A possible problem is that visitors may not know which vendor stalls are available or where they are located. Stall information may be shared only through social media post like Facebook, posters, or just word of mouth. This can make it difficult for customers to find local products and may cause vendors to lose potential sales. This is an assumption.   

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
A mobile-friendly website called "Pwesto Finder." It would display a simple vendor map (e.g. in Mugna), stall names, product categories, operating hours, and opening status. 

**How would it help the intended users?**  
The website would help visitors quickly find stalls while helping vendors become more visible.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. Users can view a stall's location, operating hours, and directions within the area.
2. Users can search for vendors by product category such as food, drinks, souvenirs, etc.

### Quality 1: Low-data and mobile-friendly
